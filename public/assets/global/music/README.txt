Background music for the scrapbook (Phase 16)
=============================================

The site plays local audio. Two tracks, both looped automatically:

  tone.mp3   The gentle pre-song BGM. (Currently in use.)
             Mixkit - "Love in the Air" by Ahjay Stelino, a slow
             piano-and-strings love theme (Mixkit Stock Music Free
             License - https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree).
             Fades in when the padlock is unlocked (Page 1) and loops
             through the spread, cake, gift choice/menu and Gifts 1-2,
             until the full song crossfades in on Gift 3.

  song.mp3   The full song. (Currently in use - user-supplied.)
             Fades in when the Gift 3 surprise page opens and keeps
             playing through the diary, memories, photo reel and final
             letter.

Notes
-----
- Keep the files reasonably small (ideally a few MB each) so they load quickly
  on a phone. Trimming/compressing to ~128 kbps MP3 is plenty for this.
- tone.mp3 is a royalty-free download, so it is committed with the project.
  song.mp3 is intentionally user-supplied: add only audio you have the right
  to use, and no copyrighted recording was downloaded or bundled for it.
- Want different filenames or formats (e.g. .ogg/.m4a)? Edit MUSIC_SOURCES in
  src/components/audio/music.ts.
